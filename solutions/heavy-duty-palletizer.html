Heavy Duty Palletizer + Squaring System

A heavy-duty robotic palletizing platform engineered for high-speed converting lines and multi-output configurations where stack precision and cycle reliability are non-negotiable.

Industrial Overview

High-speed FFG and RDC outputs above 400 bundles/min demand palletizing architectures that sustain cycle rate under variable bundle patterns, while preserving pallet geometry and downstream handling quality.

Product Description

The system combines robotic load forming, controlled squaring and conveyor synchronization to deliver robust stacking performance in demanding corrugated end-of-line environments.

Key Features

  • Tailor-made load former strategy for FFG and RDC outputs.
  • Robotic single-bundle and double-bundle palletizing modes.
  • Integrated squaring logic for shipment-grade pallet consistency.
  • Designed for high-throughput and multi-output line scenarios.
  • Industrial conveyor and robot coordination for stable cycle flow.

2 Digital Twin Configuration

Discrete Event Model Built on Real Production Behavior

The model reproduces reel flows from warehouse release to corrugator consumption and return. Simulation logic combines deterministic constraints and stochastic behavior to represent realistic intralogistics variability.

  • Discrete Event Simulation architecture with event-driven queue orchestration.
  • Random failures for transport assets and exchange stations.
  • Peak demand periods tied to production mix and speed ramps.
  • Warehouse preparation delays and release variability.
  • Double reel change events with synchronized delivery contention.
  • Dynamic queue priorities for starvation prevention.
  • Real production calendar including planned downtime windows.

Executive Conclusion

Executive Statement

The Digital Twin demonstrates that the proposed INGETRANS configuration provides sufficient logistics capacity to sustain current production while maintaining significant reserve capacity for future expansion. The simulation predicts substantial improvements in equipment availability, logistics reliability and operational efficiency, with a 50% reduction in forklift resources and virtually eliminating reel starvation under the analysed production scenarios.

Technical Appendix

Simulation Assumptions and Benchmark Inputs

  • Cycle times calibrated from INGETRANS reference installations and measured handling phases.
  • Failure distributions represented through stochastic event generation at asset and node level.
  • Warehouse release behavior modelled with delay probability and queue discipline constraints.
  • Production benchmark based on observed operating calendar and shift-level demand variance.
  • Validation focused on starvation resilience, queue saturation and transport service continuity.

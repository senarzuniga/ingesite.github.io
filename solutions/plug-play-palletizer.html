Plug & Play EasyPack + Palletizer

A compact robotic end-of-line platform designed to reduce CAPEX, eliminate civil works and deliver fast-start palletizing with the real throughput range required in corrugated plants.

Industrial Overview

This solution is positioned as a practical alternative to oversized premium palletizers, with a clear strategy: lower investment, lower mechanical complexity, high flexibility and faster ROI.

Product Description

The package integrates industrial KUKA robotics, dedicated gripping, stack squaring and interlayer handling in a self-contained Plug & Play cell. It is prepared for quick installation and easy relocation between lines.

Key Features

  • Installation in around 3 hours with only 380V supply and compressed air.
  • No civil works, foundations or long production shutdowns.
  • Forklift-movable equipment for multi-line deployment models.
  • Graphic 3D programming and operator-friendly recipe setup.
  • Automatic interlayer, top board and base board insertion.

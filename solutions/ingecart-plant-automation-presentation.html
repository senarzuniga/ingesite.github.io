INGECART

Engineering Integration for the Modernization of Corrugated Board Plants

The competitiveness of a corrugated board plant no longer depends solely on the performance of individual machines. Today, the greatest efficiency losses occur between production processes: reel logistics, internal material handling, waste management, end-of-line automation, traceability, and data-driven decision-making.

INGECART designs, integrates and commissions complete industrial architectures that optimize material flow, stabilize production and enable the technological evolution of existing plants. Our objective is not to add more automation. Our objective is to make the entire factory operate as one integrated production system.

Our Mission

Design, integrate and deliver complete industrial solutions that improve material flow, increase productivity and prepare factories for future challenges.

RFID Reel Management System

RFID reel control systems for corrugated plants automate real-time tracking, eliminate inventory errors and optimize paper consumption at corrugator level.

Industrial Overview

Paper reels are heavy, high-value assets with critical variance in grammage, width and paper type. RFID replaces manual barcode handling by using smart tags and radiofrequency readers for non-contact identification.

Product Description

The solution integrates RFID tags, lift-truck readers, dock portals and mill-roll-stand identification points to maintain real-time reel visibility from inbound reception to production consumption and remnant return.

Key Features

  • Exact real-time reel location inside warehouse stacks.
  • Automatic inbound and outbound registration through RFID portals.
  • Instant reel identification at mill roll stand positions.
  • Automatic remnant calculation after partial consumption.
  • FIFO and FEFO operational guidance for optimized reel selection.

Intralogistics Automatic Transfer & Flow Management Software

A complete intralogistics layer combining an automatic transfer car (shuttle), automatic conveyor lines and flow management software. The system moves full board stacks and master pallets between production lines without operator intervention, coordinating every load, travel and discharge cycle through PLC interlocks and plant-level dispatching logic.

Industrial Overview

In corrugated and packaging plants, transfer between lines is usually the point where flow becomes manual, unpredictable and operator dependent. The transfer car runs on a rail along the aisle, receives loads from an emitting conveyor and delivers them to the requesting destination line, while the flow management software decides priorities, sequencing and interlock permissions in real time.

Product Description

The solution has three integrated elements: the transfer car with a motorized roller bed, the fixed automatic conveyor network with load and discharge requests, and the flow management software that arbitrates the traffic, synchronizes speeds and reports status to MES/WMS. The result is a continuous, self-governed material flow with millimetric stop accuracy and full traceability of each movement.

Key Features

  • Rail-guided transfer car with motorized roller bed synchronized to fixed conveyor speed.
  • Automatic load and discharge requests exchanged between transfer car and fixed lines.
  • Transfer interlock: rollers only start once the car confirms perfect alignment with the destination conveyor.
  • Millimetric positioning via rail encoder, laser rangefinder or BPS barcode positioning.
  • Safety area scanners with warning zone (deceleration) and emergency zone (immediate braking).
  • Flow management software with priority handling, traffic arbitration and movement traceability.
  • Fieldbus integration (Profinet / EtherCAT) with variable frequency drives for controlled acceleration ramps.
  • Status reporting to MES/WMS: available, loaded, in fault, low battery.

Control Architecture: Typical I/O

The transfer car PLC is the decision node between onboard sensors, the fixed conveyor lines and the plant control system.

Typical Inputs (to the Transfer PLC)

[Car Sensors] ──> ( INPUTS ) ──> [Transfer PLC]

  • Load position sensors: photocells on the roller bed reporting "load centered", "load at front end" or "load at rear end".
  • Safety laser / area scanner: "warning zone interrupted" (car decelerates) and "emergency zone interrupted" (immediate brake lock).
  • Rail encoder / laser rangefinder: continuous signal with exact car position in millimeters along the aisle.
  • Discharge request (fixed line): wireless or infrared signal from the destination conveyor: "I am empty, you can discharge here".
  • Load request (fixed line): signal from the emitting conveyor: "a block is ready, come and collect it".
  • Physical limit switches: mechanical end-of-track switches preventing collision in case of software failure.

Typical Outputs (from the Transfer PLC)

[Transfer PLC] ──> ( OUTPUTS ) ──> [Motors / Signal Towers / Fixed Conveyors]

  • Travel direction and speed: analog or fieldbus output (Profinet / EtherCAT) to the travel motor VFD (forward / reverse / speed).
  • Roller bed rotation: command to rotate clockwise (load) or counterclockwise (discharge).
  • Electromechanical brake: engage or release the physical travel motor brake on stop.
  • Signal tower and alarm: strobe light and intermittent horn active while the car is in motion.
  • Transfer permission (interlock to fixed line): "I am perfectly aligned, start your rollers and I will start mine".
  • Car status to central system (MES/WMS): available, loaded, in fault or low battery for wireless AGV configurations.

SR-1400: Unified Solution for Collecting and Transporting All Waste

SR-1400 is positioned as a high-reliability waste logistics platform for corrugated plants, engineered to improve uptime, reduce maintenance burden, and stabilize production through continuous controlled scrap evacuation.

Industrial Overview

Most plants treat waste transport as a secondary utility, yet recurring stoppages, dust, maintenance interventions and fire-risk exposure frequently originate in this layer. SR-1400 transforms that weak point into a resilient, engineered logistics backbone.

Product Description

The system integrates collection, transfer and evacuation functions with reliability-centered mechanical design, including reinforced fixation concepts, wear-mitigation strategies and sealing-oriented engineering to reduce total cost of ownership over long operating cycles.

Key Features

  • Continuous, unified waste evacuation across corrugator and converting outputs.
  • Reliability-focused mechanical architecture for reduced failure incidence.
  • Wear and sealing strategy to reduce dust-related and friction-related degradation.
  • Low-intervention operating model with simplified maintenance routines.
  • Integration-ready design for brownfield modernization programs.

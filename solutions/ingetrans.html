INGETRANS — Intralogistics Automated Reel Feeding System

Automated corrugator paper reel handling, transfer and return

INGETRANS is a turnkey, rail-guided intralogistics solution that automates the transport, delivery and return of paper reels between warehouse and multiple corrugator consumption points.

Contact INGECART Download brochure

Overview

INGETRANS moves paper reels automatically from the warehouse exchange zone to the corrugator feed points and returns unused or partially used reels to their storage locations.

Key Functions

  • Automated Movement: Transport reels from exchange zone to corrugator without manual intervention.
  • Automated Delivery: Precise delivery to multiple consumption points via dedicated tracks.
  • Automated Return: Unused or partially used reels are returned automatically to assigned warehouse locations.
  • Dual Reel Handling: Transfer carriage can carry up to two reels simultaneously.
  • Process Integration: Works with production planning and MES for just‑in‑time delivery.
  • Continuous Supply: Production‑synchronised delivery maximises real-line capacity and minimises downtime.

How It Works (Summary)

  1. Warehouse exchange zone: Reels staged and positioned for transfer.
  2. Transfer carriage pick‑up: Elevates and secures one or two reels (≈6 s cycle).
  3. Transport: Accelerates (4.5 s ramp) to nominal speed and follows floor rails to delivery tracks.
  4. Delivery: Positions reel at track and discharges to track conveyor (≈6 s drop‑off).
  5. Simultaneous return (optional): Carriage can collect partially used reels on return trips.
  6. Automatic return to warehouse: Unused/semi‑used reels are returned to assigned storage locations.

Key Benefits

  • Eliminates forklift traffic in corrugator areas to reduce the main source of accidents.
  • Lowers accident risk and protects personnel operating near machines.
  • Reduces labor for manual reel handling and frees operators for higher‑value tasks.
  • Minimises downtime with production‑synchronised reel delivery.
  • Fast ROI (typical payback < 18 months) through efficiency gains and lower accident costs.
  • Increases OEE by maintaining continuous line capacity.

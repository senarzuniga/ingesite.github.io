Direction & Audit

Independent consulting for the corrugated and packaging industry. INGECART audits are technical and industrial diagnostics designed to convert operational observations into system-level decisions. The objective is not only to detect defects, but to identify lost capacity, unstable flow, weak maintenance discipline, misalignment between machine and logistics, and the real root cause behind low OEE, blocking, starvation, waste and low productivity.

Why This Service Matters

Complete independence from equipment suppliers, deep corrugated expertise and actionable recommendations focused entirely on customer success. The underlying principle is simple: recover capacity before installing more automation.

Audit Scope

Technical audit, flow audit, maintenance audit, OEE and productivity assessment, WIP and logistics audit, and automation readiness review.

Key Analysis Layers

1. Mechanical and physical condition

  • Alignment and parallelism.
  • Roller condition and compensation.
  • Clearances and wear.
  • Transmission and supports.
  • Condition of clichés, plates, inks, corrugation equipment, gluing and pre-heaters.

2. Process and stability

  • Real sustainable speed.
  • Setup time and reaction time.
  • Micro-stops and blocked time.
  • Starvation and outfeed pressure.
  • Frequent rework and repeated manual corrections.

3. Flow and WIP management

  • Buffer pressure and queue time.
  • Blocking in output or input positions.
  • WIP saturation and empty-position starvation.
  • Routing and destination reservation logic.
  • Need for AMR, tridents or transfer systems.

4. Control and automation maturity

  • PLC, VFD, encoder, scanners and safety condition.
  • Interlocks and sequence logic.
  • Manual versus automated decisions.
  • Integration with MES/WMS/ERP and OT/IT layers.

5. Maintenance discipline

  • Preventive versus corrective ratio.
  • MTBF and MTTR.
  • Spare parts availability.
  • Task completion rate and backlog.
  • Preventive ownership and follow-up.

6. Human factor and operating competence

  • Operator skill level.
  • Ability to adjust and hold speed without destabilizing flow.
  • Training level, preventive supervision and standard work.
  • Dependency on a key operator or supervisor.

How It Is Measured

DomainIndicators
Capacity and flowGood boxes/hour, bundles/hour, pallets/hour, tons/month, m²(ft²)/hour, blocked minutes per shift, starvation minutes per shift, rehandling events, clearance and ready-to-release time.
Quality and lossesDefects per m², waste and scrap by section, rejected material by cause, setup time and scrap during recipe change, recurring issues such as plate curvature, cliché condition, ink quality and alignment drift.
MaintenanceMTBF and MTTR by asset, preventive task completion rate, repeat failure rate, lead time for critical spare parts, reactive versus preventive maintenance cost.
OEE frameworkOEE = Availability × Performance × Quality, analyzed by machine, line/process zone, product family or SKU mix, shift and section, and for the flow zone including logistics and WIP.

Examples of Audit Findings

Operational audit (corrugator and dry-end)

  • Misalignment detected across equipment.
  • Issues with paper-path rollers and drying table skates.
  • Non-operational control system on a critical section.
  • Lack of preventive maintenance and spare parts.
  • Intervention split into immediate repairs and planned shutdown scope.
  • Training and remote support required.
  • OEE and production losses driven by technical drift and maintenance backlog.

Wet-end technical audit

  • Parallelism deviations on fluting and liner sections.
  • Loose handling and poor contact behavior in the pre-heater area.
  • Poor calibration of rollers and bearings.
  • Risk of frequent breakage and unstable process.
  • Realignment, mechanical repair, centerline verification and sensor calibration required.

Best Practice Rules

  • A machine must be evaluated as part of a flow, not as an isolated asset.
  • Every finding must be evidence-based.
  • Every recommendation must include expected KPI impact.
  • Every manual workaround is treated as a symptom of a structural problem.
  • Every investment decision must be reconciled against OEE, throughput and maintenance cost.
  • Every audit ends with a practical implementation plan, not only a technical narrative.

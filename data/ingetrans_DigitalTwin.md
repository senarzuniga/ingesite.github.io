# DIGITAL TWIN SIMULATION REPORT

## Benchmark Simulation – Forklift Logistics vs. INGETRANS Automated Reel Feeding

### A Typical European Corrugated Plant

---

# Executive Summary

To quantify the operational impact of automated reel logistics, INGECART developed a Digital Twin simulation comparing two alternative operating scenarios under identical production conditions.

Both simulations use exactly the same:

* Plant layout
* Production schedule
* Order mix
* Corrugator configuration
* Reel characteristics
* Working calendar

The only variable modified is the reel logistics system.

**Scenario A**

Conventional forklift-based reel handling.

**Scenario B**

INGETRANS fully automated reel transport system.

The objective is to evaluate how logistics continuity influences production performance, equipment utilization and operating costs.

---

# Production Continuity

The simulation demonstrates that production gains are not generated by increasing machine speed.

Both scenarios operate at exactly the same corrugator speed.

The improvement comes from maintaining continuous reel availability at every roll stand.

By eliminating waiting times and synchronizing deliveries with production consumption, the corrugator spends significantly less time waiting for paper.

# Why INGETRANS Improves Performance

Unlike conventional forklift logistics, INGETRANS continuously synchronizes reel transport with production demand.

This allows:

* Continuous reel availability.
* Automatic reel replenishment.
* Automatic reel return.
* Elimination of forklift traffic around the corrugator.
* Reduced operator dependency.
* Lower congestion.
* Higher production stability.
* Improved plant safety.

The result is not a faster machine, but a machine that spends significantly more time producing.

# Digital Twin Technology

The simulation is powered by INGECART's Discrete Event Digital Twin engine.

The model reproduces:

* Individual reel lifecycle.
* Warehouse logistics.
* Transfer carriage movements.
* Forklift operations.
* Production orders.
* Resource allocation.
* Equipment failures.
* Maintenance events.
* Random operational disturbances.
* Financial impact.

Every reel, movement, resource and event is simulated independently, allowing accurate comparison between logistics alternatives before any investment is made.

Executive Summary

INGECART developed a high-fidelity Discrete Event Digital Twin to quantify the operational and financial impact of automating paper reel logistics. This report presents a direct benchmark comparing conventional forklift-based handling against the INGETRANS automated rail-guided system, under identical production conditions.

The simulation proves that production gains are not driven by increasing corrugator speed—which remains constant in both scenarios—but by eliminating waiting times and ensuring continuous reel availability at every roll stand.

System Description

INGETRANS is a turnkey, rail-guided intralogistics solution that automates the transport, delivery, and return of paper reels between the warehouse and multiple corrugator consumption points. It eliminates forklift dependency in the corrugator area, improves safety, optimizes material flow, and integrates with production planning and MES systems.

Key Functions

  • Automated Movement: Transport reels from the exchange zone to the corrugator without manual intervention.
  • Automated Delivery: Precise delivery to multiple consumption points (single facers, roll stands) via dedicated tracks.
  • Automated Return: Unused or partially used reels are returned automatically to assigned warehouse locations.
  • Dual Reel Handling: The transfer carriage can carry up to two reels simultaneously.
  • Process Integration: Works with production planning and MES for just-in-time delivery.
  • Continuous Supply: Production-synchronized delivery maximizes real-line capacity and minimizes downtime.

How It Works (Simulated Logic)

  1. Warehouse exchange zone: Reels are staged and positioned for transfer.
  2. Transfer carriage pick up: Elevates and secures one or two reels (~6 s cycle).
  3. Transport: Accelerates (4.5 s ramp) to nominal speed and follows floor rails to delivery tracks.
  4. Delivery: Positions the reel at the track and discharges it to the track conveyor (~6 s drop-off).
  5. Simultaneous return (optional): The carriage can collect partially used reels on return trips.
  6. Automatic return to warehouse: Unused/semi-used reels are returned to assigned storage locations.

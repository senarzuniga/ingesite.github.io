DIGITAL TWIN SIMULATION REPORT

Benchmark Simulation – Forklift Logistics vs. INGETRANS Automated Reel Feeding

A Typical European Corrugated Plant

Executive Summary

To quantify the operational impact of automated reel logistics, INGECART developed a Digital Twin simulation comparing two alternative operating scenarios under identical production conditions.

Both simulations use exactly the same:

  • Plant layout
  • Production schedule
  • Order mix
  • Corrugator configuration
  • Reel characteristics
  • Working calendar

Scenario A
Conventional forklift-based reel handling.

Scenario B
INGETRANS fully automated reel transport system.

The objective is to evaluate how logistics continuity influences production performance, equipment utilization and operating costs.

Production Continuity

The simulation demonstrates that production gains are not generated by increasing machine speed. Both scenarios operate at exactly the same corrugator speed. The improvement comes from maintaining continuous reel availability at every roll stand. By eliminating waiting times and synchronizing deliveries with production consumption, the corrugator spends significantly less time waiting for paper.

Conclusion

The benchmark simulation demonstrates that automated reel logistics can significantly improve production continuity without increasing corrugator speed.

  • +7.3% annual production
  • +6 OEE points
  • 87% fewer starvation events
  • 76% less logistics-related downtime
  • More than four million additional production meters per year
  • Over €300,000 in annual operating savings

Actual project results depend on plant layout, production mix, labour costs, operating practices and existing logistics performance. For this reason, INGECART calibrates every Digital Twin using each customer's production data before preparing the final investment proposal.
